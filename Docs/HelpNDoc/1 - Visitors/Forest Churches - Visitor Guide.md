# Welcome to Forest Churches

Forest Churches helps you find a church near you in the Forest of Dean and the surrounding area. For each church you can see service times, upcoming events, facilities, contact details and directions.

You don't need an account to use the site. Everything in this guide is open to everyone.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for visitors: anyone looking for a church, a service or an event. If you look after a church's page, see the **Church Administrator Guide**. If you run the website, see the **Site Administrator Guide**.

## Finding your way around

Every page has the same header:

- **Forest Churches** (top left) takes you back to the home page.
- **Find a church** opens the church search.
- **Bulletin** shows the latest weekly pew sheet or announcement.
- **About Us** and **Contact Us** tell you more about the site and how to reach us.
- **Language** lets you read the site in another language.
- The **sun/moon** button switches between light and dark mode.
- **Church login** is for church administrators only. Visitors don't need it.

On a phone, the links are tucked behind the **Menu** button.

The footer at the bottom of every page links to the **Privacy** policy, the **FAQ** and **Contact Us**.

# Finding a church

## Searching by postcode or town

1. On the home page (or **Find a church**), type your postcode or town into **Postcode or town**.
2. Choose how far you're happy to travel from the **Within** list: 2, 5, 10, 20 or 50 miles.
3. Select **Search**.

The results list every open church within that distance, nearest first, with its distance in miles.

## Using your location

Instead of typing a postcode, select **Use My Location**. Your browser will ask whether to share your location. If you allow it, the site fills in your address for you. Then select **Search**.

If you decline, or your browser can't find you, just type your postcode instead.

> Your location is only used to fill in the search box. It isn't stored.

## Filtering the results

Select **More filters** to narrow the search:

- **Denomination**: show only churches of one denomination, or leave it on **Any Denomination**.
- **Must have**: tick any facilities you need. Only churches that have **all** the ticked facilities are shown. The choices are wheelchair access, parking, hearing aid support (hearing loop), refreshments, children's ministry, restrooms, Wi-Fi, kitchen, community hall, worship hall and outdoor space.

Every filter starts unticked, so you see all the churches near you. If a search finds nothing, try a larger distance or untick some filters.

## Reading the results

Each church in the results shows:

- its name, denomination and distance from you
- its **next service**
- the main facilities as small labels
- **View church**, which opens its full page
- **Directions**, which opens a map with directions in a new tab
- **Download details** and **Email me the details**, to keep the church's information for later (see "Saving a church's details")

On larger screens a map beside the list shows where each church is. The pins are numbered in the same order as the list; point at (or tap) a pin to see the church's name.

# A church's page

## What's on a church page

Open a church from the search results, from the home page, or from a link you've been sent. Its page shows:

- **Key facts**: denomination, typical Sunday attendance, whether the church is open, and its next service.
- **Sunday Services**: every regular service with its time, in 24-hour format (for example 10:30).
- **About us**: the church's own description.
- **Upcoming events**: events the church has listed. Select **Read more** to see the full description.
- **What to expect**: the church's facilities.
- **Find us**: the address and a map. **Directions** opens a route in a new tab.
- **Website** and **ChurchSuite** buttons, if the church has them.

If the page says **Church not found**, the link is out of date. Search for the church instead.

## Contacting a church

Use the message form on the church's page:

1. Enter **Your name** and **Your email**.
2. Add a **Subject** and **Your Message**.
3. Select **Send Message**.

Your message goes straight to the church, and they'll reply to the email address you gave.

## Saving a church's details

In the **Save these details** box on the church's page (and on each search result):

- **Download details** saves the church's description, address, phone, website, facilities and service times as a small text file.
- **Email me the details** sends the same information to your inbox. Enter your email address and select the button. A message at the top of the box confirms it's on its way.

If the email doesn't arrive within about 10 minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

# Events

## Finding events near you

On **Find a church**, scroll to **Events near me**:

1. Enter your **Postcode** (or select **Use My Location**).
2. Choose a **Church**, or leave it as **Any Church**.
3. Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date**.
4. Select **Find events**.

Each event shows its date, start and end times, and where it's being held. Select **Read more** for the full description.

The home page also shows a few upcoming events under **What's on across the Forest**.

## Why an event might disappear

One-off events are removed about 24 hours after they finish. Repeating events (weekly, monthly and so on) stay listed. If an event you expected has gone, contact the church directly.

# The weekly bulletin

## Reading the bulletin

Select **Bulletin** in the header to read the current pew sheet or announcement. A full weekly bulletin has tabs:

- **Notices**: this week's notices and a pastoral note.
- **Readings**: the first reading and the Gospel.
- **Hymns**: the hymns for the service.
- **Collect**: the collect (prayer) for the week.

Select **Download PDF** for the printable version, or **Print** to print the page. When a new bulletin is published, a banner at the top of the site links to it.

# Personalising the site

## Changing the language

Open the **Language** menu in the header and choose English, Čeština, Deutsch, Français, فارسی (Farsi), ไทย (Thai) or Українська (Ukrainian). The site remembers your choice. Farsi is shown right-to-left.

Churches write their own descriptions and event details, so those stay in the language the church wrote them in.

## Light and dark mode

Select the **sun/moon** button in the header to switch between light and dark mode. The site remembers your choice on that device. Until you choose, it follows your device's own setting.

# Help and contact

## Frequently asked questions

The **FAQ** page (link in the footer) answers common questions, such as how churches join, where data is stored and why events disappear.

## Contacting Forest Churches

To ask about the site, suggest a church, or report something wrong:

1. Select **Contact Us**.
2. Fill in your name, email, subject and message.
3. Select **Send Message**.

To contact a particular church, use the form on that church's page instead.

## Privacy

The **Privacy** page explains what information the site collects and how it's used. In short, your search location isn't stored, and contact details you enter are only used to send your message or the details you asked for.

## Troubleshooting

- **No churches found**: choose a larger distance, untick some filters, or check the postcode is spelled correctly.
- **Use My Location does nothing**: your browser may be blocking location. Allow it in your browser's settings, or type your postcode.
- **The map doesn't show**: the list still works. Check your internet connection and refresh the page.
- **An emailed link doesn't work**: most links in our emails expire after 24 hours. Request the information again.
- **I didn't receive an email**: wait 10 minutes (emails are sent in batches every 5 minutes), then check your spam folder. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us.
