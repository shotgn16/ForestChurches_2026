# Welcome, church administrators

This guide is for people who look after a church's page on Forest Churches. Your account has the **AuthorizedChurch** role, so after you sign in you get **Church admin**: a set of pages for keeping your church's details, services and events up to date.

Visitors don't need an account. Everything they see comes from what you enter here, so keeping it current is the most useful thing you can do.

## What you can do

- Edit your church's public page: name, description, address, contact details and facilities.
- List your regular service times.
- Upload a logo or photo.
- Add, edit and delete events, or sync them automatically from ChurchSuite.
- Mark your church as closed (hidden from search) and reopen it.
- Manage your own sign-in: password, two-factor authentication and personal data.

# Getting started

## How churches join

Forest Churches is invitation only, so not just anyone can create a church listing.

1. **Ask for an invitation.** Use **Contact Us** on the site, or the **ask for an invite** link on the Church login page. The site administrators will send an invitation to your email address.
2. **Open the invitation email** and select its link. The link works for 24 hours. If it has expired, ask for a new one.
3. **Create your account.** On **Create account**, enter the **same email address** the invitation was sent to, choose a password, and optionally upload a logo. Then select **Create account**.
4. **Confirm your email.** We send a confirmation email. Open it and select the link. If it doesn't arrive, use **Resend Confirmation Email** on the Church login page.
5. **Wait for approval.** A site administrator approves new accounts by giving them the AuthorizedChurch role. Until then you can sign in, but the church admin pages aren't available. You'll be able to use them as soon as you're approved.

Your church's page is created as soon as you register. Fill in the details straight away so it's useful when visitors find it.

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol. A short phrase, such as `Bluebells-in-May-2026`, is easy to remember and hard to guess.

## Signing in

1. Select **Church login** in the header.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**. Select **Show** to check what you've typed.
3. Tick **Keep me signed in** if this is your own device.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If your church uses ChurchSuite and you've connected it (see "Connecting ChurchSuite"), you can select **Sign in with ChurchSuite** instead.

If you've turned on two-factor authentication, you'll be asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app next.

## Forgotten your password

1. On the Church login page, select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter the email address you sign in with and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password. The link only works for a limited time, so use it soon.

After too many wrong passwords your account is locked for a short time. Wait a few minutes and try again.

## Finding your way around Church admin

After signing in, select **My church** (or **Church admin**) in the header. The dark bar at the top shows you're in admin mode, with links back to the public site and to **View public page**.

The menu on the left (a scrolling strip on phones) has:

- **My Church**: your church's public details, services, logo and status.
- **My Events**: your events.
- **ChurchSuite**: connecting your ChurchSuite account.
- **Bulletins**: weekly bulletins (only if your account has been given bulletin access).
- **Profile**, **Change Password**, **Two-Factor Authentication** and **My Data**: your own account.

Select **Logout** in the top bar when you've finished, especially on a shared computer.

# Your church's page

## Editing your church's details

Open **My Church**. The **Details** section controls what visitors see:

- **Church name**
- **About your church**: shown at the top of your page. Up to 1,000 characters. Say who you are, what a typical Sunday is like, and who's welcome.
- **Denomination**: used by the search filter.
- **Typical Sunday attendance**: for example `20–50`.
- **Address**: used to place you on the map and work out distances, so give the full address and postcode. **Use my current location** fills it in for you if you're at the church.
- **Phone Number**, **Website** and **ChurchSuite address**: website addresses must start with `https://`.

Select **Update Information** to save, or **Discard changes** to undo your edits. A green message at the top confirms the save.

> If your church doesn't appear in searches, check the address. The map and distances depend on it.

## Facilities

In the **Facilities** section, switch on everything your church offers: wheelchair access, parking, hearing aid support, refreshments, children's ministry, restrooms, Wi-Fi, kitchen, community hall, worship hall and outdoor space.

Visitors can filter searches by these, so only switch on facilities you really have. Select **Update Information** to save.

## Service times

The **Your Church Service Times** section lists your regular services:

1. Enter the **Time** (24-hour, for example `10:30`).
2. Enter the **Service name**, for example `Morning Communion Service`.
3. Select **Add Service**.

To remove a service, select **Remove** next to it and confirm. Services are shown on your page in time order, and the earliest upcoming one is shown as your **Next service**.

## Logo or photo

In **Logo or photo**:

1. Select **Choose an image** and pick a PNG or JPEG. A square image works best.
2. Select **Upload Logo**.

Your logo appears on your church page and in search results.

## Seeing what visitors see

Select **View public page ↗** in the top bar to open your church's page in a new tab. Check it after making changes.

## Closing or reopening your church

If your church stops holding services, open **My Church** and go to the **Danger zone**:

- **Mark church as closed…** hides your church from search and from the home page. You'll be asked to confirm.
- While closed, a notice explains that you're hidden, and **Reopen church** makes you visible again.

Your details and events are kept while you're closed.

# Events

## Viewing your events

**My Events** shows your **Upcoming events** in date order and, below them, **Past events**. Each event shows its date, times and address, plus a label: **ChurchSuite** if it came from ChurchSuite, or **Added here** if you added it on this site.

## Adding an event

1. In **My Events**, select **Add New Event**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Event name**
   - **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends** (24-hour times)
   - **Repeats**: Doesn't repeat, Daily, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Every 3 weeks, Monthly or Every 2 months
   - **Location**: the event's address
   - **Description**: what happens, who it's for and anything people should bring
   - **Link for more information** (optional): a booking or information page
   - **Image** (optional): a PNG or JPEG
3. Select **Create event**.

You'll get an email confirming the event was created.

## Editing or deleting an event

In **My Events**, select **Edit** next to the event, make your changes and select **Save changes**. If you don't choose a new image, the current one is kept.

To delete an event, select **Delete** and confirm. This can't be undone, and you'll get an email confirming the event was deleted.

## How long events stay listed

- **One-off events** are removed about 24 hours after they finish, to keep the site tidy.
- **Repeating events** stay listed. From time to time you'll get a "Do you still need this?" email. Follow its link to keep the event, or delete it if it has stopped.

## Syncing events from ChurchSuite

If you've connected ChurchSuite, your ChurchSuite events appear automatically. To pull in changes straight away, select **Sync from ChurchSuite** in **My Events**.

Edit ChurchSuite events in ChurchSuite itself. Changes made here may be overwritten at the next sync.

# Connecting ChurchSuite

## Why connect ChurchSuite

If your church uses ChurchSuite, connecting it means your events appear on your Forest Churches page automatically, and you can sign in with your ChurchSuite account.

## Setting up the connection

Open **ChurchSuite** in the Church admin menu and follow the three steps on the page:

1. In the **ChurchSuite developer console**, create a new OAuth app as a **public (PKCE) client**.
2. Copy its **Client ID** and paste it into **ChurchSuite Client ID**.
3. Select **Save & Authorize with ChurchSuite**, then approve access when ChurchSuite asks.

The page then shows **Connected**. Use **Sync now** at any time to fetch the latest events.

## Updating your details from ChurchSuite

On **My Church**, **Sync from ChurchSuite → Sync now** updates your church information from your ChurchSuite account. **Manage connection** takes you to the ChurchSuite page.

## Removing the connection

Under **Registered Logins** on the ChurchSuite page, select **Remove** next to ChurchSuite and confirm. Events already imported stay until you delete them.

# Bulletins

## Publishing a bulletin

Bulletins are only available if a site administrator has given your account bulletin access. If you can see **Bulletins** in the menu but get an "Access denied" page, ask a site administrator.

The bulletin pages work the same way for everyone. See **Bulletins** in the Site Administrator Guide for full instructions: creating a bulletin or announcement, uploading a PDF, making it live, and showing the site-wide banner.

# Your account

## Profile

**Profile** shows the email address you sign in with and your **Phone Number** (optional). Select **Save Changes** after editing.

You can't change your email address yourself, for security. Contact the site administrators if it needs to change.

## Changing your password

Open **Change Password**, enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** twice, and select **Update password**.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from your phone each time you sign in, so nobody can use your account even if they learn your password. We strongly recommend it.

To turn it on:

1. Open **Two-Factor Authentication** and select **Add authenticator app**.
2. Install an authenticator app, such as Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator, on your phone.
3. Scan the QR code with the app. If you can't scan it, type in the key shown under the code.
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and select **Verify and turn on**.
5. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe. Each code lets you sign in once if you lose your phone.

From the same page you can:

- **Reset recovery codes**: make a new set (your old codes stop working).
- **Reset authenticator app**: move 2FA to a new phone.
- **Forget this browser**: ask for a code again on this device.
- **Disable 2FA**: turn it off (not recommended).

If you've lost your phone, select **Don't have your phone? Use a recovery code** when signing in.

## Your data

**My Data** lets you:

- **Download your data**: everything we hold about you and your church, as a single file.
- **Delete your account**: permanently removes your sign-in and the data held with it. Enter your password and select **Delete permanently**. This can't be undone.

If you only want your church hidden for a while, use **Mark church as closed** instead of deleting your account.

# Help

## Emails from Forest Churches

You'll receive emails when you register, confirm your address, reset your password, create or delete an event, and when a repeating event needs checking. Emails are sent in batches every 5 minutes, so allow up to 10 minutes. Check your spam folder, and add info@forestchurches.co.uk to your contacts.

## Common problems

- **"Access denied" after signing in**: your account hasn't been approved yet, or you've opened a page for site administrators. Contact us.
- **My church doesn't appear in search**: check it isn't marked as closed and that the address and postcode are correct.
- **Someone can't find my event**: one-off events are removed about 24 hours after they finish; check the date and time.
- **An email link has expired**: most links last 24 hours, and password reset links expire sooner. Request a new one.
- **I'm locked out**: wait a few minutes after too many wrong passwords, or use **Forgot Password**.

## Getting help

Use **Contact Us** on the site and tell us your church's name and what you were trying to do.
