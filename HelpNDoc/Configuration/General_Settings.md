General Settings

Settings overview

General settings control the tenant behavior and defaults.

Common settings

- Church display name, address, timezone
- Default event capacity and registration window
- Contact routing and default recipient roles
- Email templates and branding (logo, from address)

Advanced

- Integration toggles: enable/disable OAuth connectors or webhooks
- Data retention policy: how long to keep messages and event data

Recommendation

- Review and test email branding and templates after changing the from address or logo.
