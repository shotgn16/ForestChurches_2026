Sample Scenarios

1. Newcomer sign-up
- A visitor registers for a Sunday event. The system sends a welcome email and adds them to a follow-up segment.

2. Volunteer recruitment
- Create an event labeled Volunteer Training, tag registrants as volunteers, and send a bulk follow-up to confirm roles.

3. Capacity overflow
- Enable waitlist; when a spot opens, the first waitlist registrant receives an auto-notification and confirmation link.

4. Emergency cancellation
- Cancel event, trigger cancellation email to registrants, and log cancellation reason in audit trail.
