Advanced Tasks

Tasks that require higher privileges or additional care

- Bulk imports/exports: importing users or historical registrations
- Event transfers: moving events between organizers or tenants (admin-only)
- Custom registration fields: creating complex forms and validation rules
- Integrations: setting up OAuth connectors and webhook endpoints

Guidance

- Perform large imports during off-peak hours and after backups.
- Document any manual data-editing steps for auditability.
