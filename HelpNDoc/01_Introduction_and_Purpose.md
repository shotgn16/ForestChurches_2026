Introduction & Purpose of ForestChurches

Purpose

ForestChurches is a hosted community management application built for small-to-medium churches and groups. Its goal is to simplify event management, communication, member and organization administration, and secure file handling. The system is optimized for volunteers and admins with limited technical experience.

Audience

- Church administrators and staff
- Volunteers who manage events or communications
- Congregants who register for events and contact their local church
- System administrators and IT staff who host or maintain the service

Key benefits

- Centralized event creation and sign-up
- Integrated email and group communication tools
- Fine-grained user and church-level permissions
- Simple onboarding workflow for new churches and users
- Designed for secure document uploads (e.g., PDFs) and attachments with original filenames preserved

How to use this help set
