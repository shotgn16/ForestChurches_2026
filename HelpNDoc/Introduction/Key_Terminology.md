Key Terminology

Tenant / Church

A tenant represents a church or organization using the system. Data is scoped by TenantId.

User Roles

- PlatformAdmin: global platform-level privileges
- ChurchAdmin: manages a specific church tenant
- EventOrganizer: creates and manages events
- Volunteer / Member: limited permissions for events and communication

Event

A scheduled gathering with metadata (time, location, capacity) and registration options.

Registrant

A person who signs up for an event. May be pending approval or confirmed.

Attachment / Upload

Files stored alongside events or messages; original filenames are preserved.

Whitelist

A list of approved domains or churches that can sign-up without manual vetting.

Audit Trail

Immutable logging of important actions for compliance and troubleshooting.
