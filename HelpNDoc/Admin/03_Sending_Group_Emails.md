Sending group emails

Overview

Admins and church users with the appropriate role can send group emails to members, volunteers, or custom groups. This article covers how to create, target, preview, and send group messages.

Steps to send a group email

1. Compose
- From the Communications or Email section, click New Message. Enter subject, body, and optionally choose a template.
- Use merge fields for personalization (e.g., {{FirstName}}, {{EventTitle}}).

2. Targeting
- Select recipients by role, group, tag, or manual list of emails.
- Preview the recipient count and ensure segments are correct.

3. Attachments
- Attach documents if required. The system preserves original filenames when storing and sending attachments.

4. Preview & send
- Preview the email and send a test email to an address you control.
- Use scheduled send if you want delivery at a specific time.

5. Tracking & logs
- After sending, review email history and delivery logs for bounces. Retry failed deliveries when appropriate.

Best practices

- Use templates for recurring messages to save time and maintain consistency.
- Segment emails to reduce noise and increase relevance.
- Respect subscriber preferences and include a clear unsubscribe or contact option where applicable.

Troubleshooting

- If emails are not delivered, confirm SMTP/provider credentials and check for bounce reports.
- Check email templates for missing merge fields that might cause rendering issues.

If the site is multi-tenant, sending limits or rate limits may apply; check platform policies before sending large batches.
