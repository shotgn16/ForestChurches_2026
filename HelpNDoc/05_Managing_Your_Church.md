Managing your church, data and events

Overview

This article covers day-to-day management tasks for church administrators: user and role management, data exports, event lifecycle, attendance, and routine maintenance.

User and role management

- Invite users: From the People or Users screen, send an invite to staff or volunteers. Assign roles immediately to grant appropriate permissions.
- Edit roles: Use role management to add/remove permissions. Common roles: ChurchAdmin, EventOrganizer, Volunteer, Member.
- Deactivate users: Instead of deleting, deactivate accounts to preserve historical data and audit logs.

Events lifecycle

- Create & draft: Create event records and save as draft while setting details.
- Publish: Make an event visible to the intended audience.
- Registration management: View registrants, approve or reject if manual approval is required, and export registrant lists.
- Capacity handling: Enable waitlists for full events and automatically promote from waitlist when spots free up.
- Recurring events: Use the recurrence options for repeated weekly or monthly items. Edit series or individual occurrences.
- Cancellation: When cancelling, use the cancellation workflow to notify registered attendees.

Attendance and check-in

- Check-in: Use the event check-in UI or barcode/QR workflows if available. Mark attendance to update reporting.
- Bulk updates: For large services, use the CSV import/check-in features.

Data management and exports

- Export attendees: Export a CSV for registration lists, sign-in sheets, and analytics.
- Backup policy: Ensure regular backup of tenant data and uploaded files if self-hosted.
- Data retention: Use retention policies for old events and logs; archive or purge according to your policy and legal obligations.

Communications

- Email lists: Create groups and segments for targeted communication (e.g., volunteers, youth leaders, newcomers).
- Templates: Maintain templates for common messages (confirmation, reminder, cancellation).
- Audit: Review email logs to confirm delivery and track bounces; correct invalid addresses.

Document storage

- Uploads: Keep uploaded documents organized by event or folder. Filenames are preserved to make tracking and retrieval simpler.
- Access control: Limit who can view or download attachments; use role-based access for sensitive documents.

Routine maintenance

- Review user access quarterly and remove outdated accounts.
- Test SMTP and scheduled jobs after any configuration changes.
- Monitor event capacity, recurring event schedules, and address duplicates.

For operational questions or when special integrations are required, contact the technical administrator or the ForestChurches support team.
